# From Assistant to Collaborator: How Claude 3.7 Sonnet is Changing AI Product Development

## Hybrid Reasoning Model: Fast and Slow Thinking AI

### Revolutionary Design of Dual-Mode Thinking

Anthropic's latest release, Claude 3.7 Sonnet, represents a major breakthrough in AI assistant technology. As a hybrid reasoning model, it can flexibly switch between immediate responses and deep thinking depending on the task requirements. This design stands in stark contrast to other models in the market that focus on a single thinking mode, providing users with an experience that better aligns with human thought processes.

The design philosophy of this model is similar to human fast and slow cognitive modes. Some questions require immediate answers based on knowledge and intuition, while others need a more in-depth thinking process. To achieve this goal, Anthropic has introduced a toggle for "Extended Thinking" mode in both the web interface and API, allowing users to decide the appropriate thinking mode based on specific contexts.

### Product Philosophy Driven by Practicality

Another key feature of Claude 3.7 Sonnet is its practicality-oriented approach. Unlike many models in the market that focus on solving math competitions or computer science problems, this model emphasizes applications in real work scenarios. The model is optimized for real-world tasks, particularly common uses of large language models in the workplace.

This design not only allows users to adjust the AI's thinking style according to their needs but also provides API clients with the ability to find a balance between cost and depth of thinking. Users can now precisely control the time and depth of AI thinking, avoiding the unpredictability of overly long thinking processes while ensuring complex problems receive well-considered answers.

## Practical Priority: AI Designed for Real Work Scenarios

### Breakthrough Progress in Software Development

Claude 3.7 Sonnet excels in software development, bringing tremendous value to both Anthropic's internal and external customers. Based on this capability, Anthropic has launched a new product called "Claude Code," currently in research preview. This tool allows developers to delegate coding tasks directly to Claude from the terminal, significantly improving development efficiency.

Claude Code has become an integral part of Anthropic's internal workflow, with team members finding the extended thinking mode particularly valuable in coding scenarios. This tool not only improves development efficiency but also changes the way teams collaborate, making software development more fluid and rapid.

### Real-time Problem-Solving Examples

An interesting example illustrates this point well. One day, the team discovered a contrast issue with a toggle button in the UI. The developer only needed to walk a few steps to the designer's desk, jointly determine a new color scheme, and complete the fix in less than a minute. Six months ago, such a quick solution would have been unimaginable.

This change represents a fundamental transformation in workflow, demonstrating how AI can substantially change team collaboration methods. The speed of problem-solving not only improves efficiency but also greatly enhances product quality and user experience, allowing teams to focus more on innovation rather than fixing problems.

### Unexpected Visualization Capabilities

Surprisingly, Claude has also demonstrated excellent capabilities in data visualization. This ability is reflected not only in generating Mermaid diagrams and flowcharts but also in creating complete React components and clickable prototypes. Visualization is an unexpected strength in AI models, and although it wasn't specifically optimized for this function during training, the capability developed naturally.

This ability allows product managers, engineers, and even sales personnel to more effectively express and communicate their ideas. Team members use Claude's extended thinking mode to create visual content for expressing user flows in product specifications, greatly improving cross-functional communication efficiency and allowing ideas to transform from concept to reality more quickly.

## New Model of AI-Driven Product Development

### The Four-Legged Structure

Anthropic is redefining the AI product development process. In traditional product development models, there are "three pillars": product, design, and engineering. However, in the AI era, research has become an indispensable fourth pillar that needs to be continuously involved throughout the product development lifecycle.

Research teams need to treat product teams as customers, while product teams also need to work closely with research, forming a mutually beneficial relationship. This deep integration ensures that products can fully utilize the latest research results, while research directions can better target actual needs.

### Evaluation-Driven Development Approach

The development process of the style feature well illustrates the evaluation-driven development approach. Development begins with collecting customer feedback, then creating product specifications. Next, the team designs a series of evaluations, which are indicators of whether the feature is successful.

For the style feature, the team created evaluations for specific use cases, such as Claude acting as a domain expert adjusting technical explanations to match user knowledge levels, or adjusting output formats according to user preferences. These evaluations include actual user preference strings and test prompts to verify whether Claude can adjust responses according to user preferences.

### AI Version of Automated Testing

Imagine how terrible it would be if every time a website is updated, all previous features might suddenly stop working. This is why there are automated tests in software development. Anthropic has created a similar system for AI models.

The team uses evaluation results to continuously improve the model or system prompts, just like modifying code until tests pass. These evaluations come in two forms: one based on objective facts (is this answer correct?), and another based on functional purpose (does this response match the style we want?).

This system has proven its value in practice. When developing Claude 3.7, the team routinely ran evaluation tests before release and discovered that the new model had broken the user preference feature. This feature allows users to set preferences for Claude to respond in specific styles. Just like software tests finding bugs, these evaluations helped the team discover and fix problems before product launch.

This is how model CI/CD (Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment) actually works. Each time a new model or feature is developed, it's checked to ensure it doesn't break existing functionality. This approach greatly improves product reliability and allows teams to launch new features faster and with more confidence, knowing there's a safety net protecting the user experience.

## Practical Methods for AI Product Development

### Clear Vision and Self-Use

Developing AI products requires several key practices. First, there must be a clear understanding of the product vision. What problem is being solved? Who are the target customers? What are the typical use cases? The answers to these basic questions will guide the entire product development process.

The importance of using your own product cannot be overlooked, also known as "eating your own dog food." This approach applies not only to Anthropic but also to AI product teams of various sizes. Becoming an expert in your own product and understanding the factors hindering success is the foundation for building excellent products.

### Tight Feedback Loops

Successful AI product development requires establishing tight feedback loops between marketing, product, engineering, and research departments. Researchers embedded in product teams ensure smooth transformation from user feedback to research priorities.

This is a completely new way of working that requires learning new skills, but it also brings revolutionary product development methods. Team members don't need to be experts in all areas, but they need sufficient cross-disciplinary knowledge to communicate and collaborate effectively.

### AI-Assisted Product Development Process

The actual AI product development process can be assisted by Claude in creating product specifications. Starting with dictating the product vision, then developing detailed specification documents, designing an evaluation framework, and finally iterative improvement.

Teams use default product specification templates and customer feedback, and utilize Claude's dictation function to record verbal descriptions of product visions. This approach not only improves efficiency but also ensures product consistency with user needs, allowing product managers to focus more on creativity and strategy rather than document writing.

## Collapse of Talent Stack and Cross-Functional Collaboration

### Blurring of Role Boundaries

Adobe's Scott Balsky once proposed the concept of "talent stack collapse," meaning that traditionally strictly separated functional roles are merging. A similar phenomenon has emerged within Anthropic. Designers submit code pull requests, engineers create visual designs, and salespeople write product specifications.

This merger not only changes how work is done but also creates more opportunities for innovation and collaboration. Team members can cross traditional role boundaries, think about problems from multiple perspectives, and propose more comprehensive and innovative solutions.

### Breakthrough of Static Friction

This merger creates new collaboration opportunities rather than threatening functional boundaries. It's like the difference between static friction and rolling friction. Starting a project is most difficult, but once the initial resistance is overcome, progress becomes smoother.

AI tools help teams break through this "static friction," accelerating project initiation and collaboration. Even small tasks like creating a website or landing page can help teams build momentum to drive larger-scale projects forward.

### Bottom-Up Innovation Culture

In reality, this doesn't mean everyone is doing the same work, but creates more ways to break through static friction and build momentum. An example from early that morning illustrates this well. An engineer created a compelling product model that might influence the team's product decisions.

This cross-functional collaboration injects new creativity and efficiency into product development, making it easier for innovation to emerge from any team member, not just from the traditional product team. Anthropic's team culture encourages this bottom-up energy and innovation, partly due to the joining and influence of Instagram co-founder Mike Krieger.

## Claude's Evolution: From Assistant to Collaborator

### 15 Months of Amazing Development

Claude is undergoing a crucial evolution from assistant to collaborator. Looking back at Claude's development journey, from the initial small-scale Claude 2 series to today's mature product widely adopted by global enterprises, it has come a long way.

Just 15 months ago, Claude was small, had no API, no mobile application, and was unavailable in many countries. Today, it has been widely adopted and has become an important tool for global enterprises. This rapid development demonstrates the execution capability of the Anthropic team and the strong market demand for high-quality AI assistants.

### Flipping the Script: Future Vision

However, this is just the beginning. Currently, Claude is more like a capable assistant, requiring users to clearly guide it toward specific goals, providing precise prompts and information. In the coming year, Anthropic plans to "flip the script," making Claude a "capable collaborator."

This transformation will fundamentally change how users interact with AI, enabling Claude to more proactively solve problems rather than just passively responding to instructions. Users will be able to delegate truly meaningful tasks without directing every detailed step.

### Three Key Evolutionary Dimensions of the Collaborator

This evolution is likened to a promotion on the career ladder. Claude will make progress in three key dimensions:

1. **Knowledge Range**: Expanding the information horizon, understanding the user's world and environment more deeply. Claude will be able to better understand users' backgrounds and needs, providing more relevant and personalized support.
2. **Communication Ability**: Communicating with users in novel ways, providing more valuable interactions. Interactions will no longer be limited to text conversations but will be able to utilize richer media and expression methods, making collaboration more natural and efficient.
3. **Execution Capability**: Truly taking over tasks, allowing users to delegate meaningful work. Users will be able to entrust Claude with substantial work without detailed guidance for each operation, greatly reducing workload.

This transformation does not rely on a single feature but comes from the fusion of capabilities from various aspects of the model and product. The goal is to make Claude not just an assistant but a collaborative partner that can solve users' most pressing problems and substantially lighten their workload.

## Personality: Claude's Differentiating Advantage

### Multi-Model Strategy and Product Diversity

When discussing whether AI will become commoditized, several important differentiating factors are worth noting. First, enterprises are adopting multi-model strategies, choosing different AI models for different use cases. Many companies have implemented model selectors in internal tools, allowing employees to switch models based on specific task requirements.

Second, cost considerations lead companies to choose the most cost-effective solutions in different scenarios, such as using Claude family models with different performance and cost thresholds (Haiku, Sonnet, Opus). Anthropic also not only provides APIs but has developed product experiences such as Claude.ai and Claude for Work, which use their own models to provide users with more complete solutions.

### Shaping Claude's Personality Traits

However, the most notable differentiating factor is Claude's personality. Anthropic has a dedicated "Claude Character Lead," Amanda Askell, responsible for guiding Claude's personality traits, such as curiosity, truth-seeking, open-mindedness, etc. These traits make Claude a "trusted collaborator" in the eyes of users.

These personality traits are difficult to quantify or measure through standard evaluations, but they have a profound impact on user experience. They not only drive the adoption of Claude.ai and Claude for Work but are also why companies like Intercom, DoorDash, and Lyft choose Claude API.

### Importance of Emotional Connection

Why would someone become your friend? Perhaps not because they have a cool job or are good at cooking, but because you just like them. Similarly, some people just like Claude, and this emotional connection is difficult to measure with technical indicators, but it is a key driver of user choice and loyalty.

Corporate clients choose Claude API partly because they hope AI can convey a genuine relationship with end customers. In consumer products, this emotional connection is even more important, directly affecting user experience and satisfaction. Claude's personality is not just a technical feature but a product philosophy, reflecting Anthropic's profound understanding of how AI interacts with humans.
