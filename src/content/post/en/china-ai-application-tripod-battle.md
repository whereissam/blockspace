# From Quark, Yuanbao to Doubao: The Tripod Competition in China's AI Application Market and Industry Future

I recently listened to an episode of the [亂翻書](https://open.spotify.com/episode/76z1O37JPVU2k6Qlo9SPgQ?si=Jw4rgDIMQm61jn0FEflgqQ) podcast, which explored the current state of China's AI application market, with a special focus on the competitive landscape formed by three major AI products: Quark, Yuanbao, and Doubao. This episode was extremely informative, covering market structure changes, product differentiation, technological evolution, and business model innovation.

China's AI application market is at a critical turning point. The era of the Six Small Dragons has ended, and now a Three Kingdoms competitive landscape dominated by Quark, Yuanbao, and Doubao has been established. In this competition, these AI products backed by tech giants are displaying completely different positioning and strategies, reflecting their different understandings of the future of AI applications. The core of this competition is the battle for AI as a new human-machine interface.

## The Battle for AI Entry Points: The Transformation from Frame to Flow

### The UI Revolution from Mobile Internet to AI Era

One of the biggest transformations in the AI era is the fundamental change in user interfaces. In the mobile internet era, "flow" was the main UI mode, with users scrolling up and down to browse content, which was the foundation for the success of products like Douyin and Toutiao. The core interaction of "flow" was very simple - continuous scrolling, at most double-tapping or liking something, forming the core interaction between users and mobile devices.

In the AI era, "frame" is becoming the most important UI. Starting with ChatGPT, considered the most representative product of AI-native applications, it has shown everyone that today we need to interact with AI through dialogue, making the input frame the most important UI element in the AI era. Users input their needs through a dialogue box, and AI understands and executes complex tasks.

This change has disrupted the previous competitive landscape. ByteDance has absolute advantages in the "flow" area - Douyin is a great flow product, as are Toutiao and Tomato Novel - but ByteDance had no "frame" product, so they had to start from scratch with Doubao.

Tencent has a powerful "frame" product in WeChat, but WeChat's input box cannot be directly used for AI products because of significant privacy concerns. Users would not accept an AI suddenly popping up during a chat with family members saying let me help you reply to your mom's question. Additionally, WeChat has always been very restrained in product development, so even when integrating DeepSeek and Mixian models, it did so relatively subtly within the search box.

Quark from Alibaba, as a search product, naturally has the characteristic of a "frame." It has already accumulated experience in the tool mindset, and adding capabilities from Qianwen and Alibaba Cloud seems very natural. This resource endowment gave Quark an early advantage in transforming into an AI application.

### The Return and Innovation of the "Super Frame"

Quark's proposed "Super Frame" concept reminds people of Baidu's "Frame Computing" concept from 2009. At that time, Baidu hoped to aggregate services through frame computing, allowing users to input simple commands like weather or RMB exchange rates in the Baidu input box and get results directly without jumping to other websites.

However, "Frame Computing" faced many limitations at the time. It was constrained by the PC-side product form and could only make some standardized attempts, such as calculator functions. Frame computing later evolved into a "landing page" strategy, developing intermediate pages, but still struggled to achieve true one-stop service because many services required jumping to professional websites to complete.

In contrast, today's "Super Frame" has made a qualitative leap with the help of large model technology. Quark's head Wu Jia mentioned in an interview that creating Quark features in 2023 was painful because they could only abstract workflows one by one, but these scenarios were endless. With AI now, there's no need to write code or arrange people to do data labeling; AI can create by itself, with its subjective initiative emerging.

Wu Jia gave the Super Frame three labels: smart, professional, and versatile. He believes that in the past, people used search, but in the future, people will use AI, and AI will use tools. This represents a fundamental paradigm shift in search, where AI can understand users' complex natural language instructions, automatically select and call appropriate tools, and complete a variety of tasks.

## Differentiation Strategies of the Three Major Products

### DeepSeek's Special Position

Before discussing the three major products, it's worth mentioning DeepSeek's special position in China's AI market. By all rights, DeepSeek should be included in the discussion as it initiated and amplified the AI boom during this year's Spring Festival. However, when using DeepSeek, servers are often busy because it has actively given up on daily active user growth.

DeepSeek's product presentation is very different from Yuanbao, Quark, and Doubao. It's more like a minimalist product made to make it easier for people to use its model. In terms of feature richness and user experience, it doesn't even provide basic guarantees - inputs often fail to connect or disconnect. DeepSeek founder Liang Wensuo has explicitly stated that this is not his goal; he doesn't care about tens of millions of daily active users but hopes to reach AGI faster.

### Yuanbao: Pure Model Experience

Yuanbao has taken the simplest route, essentially packaging Tencent's Hunyuan large model into a dialogue interface. This pure "model-level product" positioning allows users to directly converse with the AI model and experience the model's capabilities.

Since the Spring Festival, Yuanbao has been heavily promoted throughout the WeChat ecosystem, appearing in many places within WeChat and other clients, quickly accumulating tens of millions of users. Yuanbao's positioning is very pure - providing a quality AI dialogue interface.

### Doubao: Exploration and Transformation

Doubao's product positioning is relatively complex, even with some internal contradictions. On one hand, Doubao has tried to launch features like daily role-playing, randomly pushing a character it impersonates when opening Doubao - for example, Socrates today, someone else tomorrow. One guest mentioned getting Li Mochou several times.

On the other hand, Doubao is also exploring other functional directions. A real case was mentioned where someone had their child use AI and found that among several AI products, the child particularly liked using Doubao. Initially thinking it was because the product capabilities were exceptionally strong, they later discovered it was because Doubao would recommend a Douyin short video after answering a question, and the child was actually just watching short videos.

These attempts reflect Doubao team's internal thinking and struggles, including discussions during their reviews about whether a chatbot is an appropriate product carrier, leading to numerous internal attempts and adjustments.

### Quark: Scenarios and Tools

Quark's positioning is more clear-cut, focusing on continuously expanding application scenarios. The Quark team's concept is clearer - continuously developing application scenarios such as writing presentations, papers, finding resources, etc., breaking down individual scenarios, and integrating all tool applications into complete workflows suitable for users.

Quark is the most functionality and tool-oriented of the three products, dedicated to providing diverse practical functions. This positioning is consistent with Quark's past accumulation in search and tools, and also highly aligned with Alibaba's overall AI strategy.

## AI Strategies of Big Companies

### Alibaba's Three-Layer Strategy

Quark occupies a central position in Alibaba's AI strategy. Alibaba's AI strategy is divided into three layers: the bottom layer is Alibaba Cloud providing computing power, the middle layer is the Qianwen large model, and the top layer is product applications. In this structure, Quark is positioned as the main C-end product, while DingTalk carries the B-end responsibilities.

This three-in-one structure is a common characteristic of Chinese tech giants, including Baidu, ByteDance, and Tencent - all having cloud to provide computing power, self-developed models, and products for consumer applications. None of these three layers can be missing.

In comparison, overseas tech companies have different strategies. Only Google seems to be implementing a similar three-layer structure; other companies like Amazon have cloud but no model, Microsoft has cloud but uses OpenAI's model, while OpenAI itself has no cloud, but has models and products. Chinese tech giants generally tend to build complete technology stacks, forming their unique ecosystem barriers.

### Baidu's Strategic Adjustment

Baidu has also undergone a significant transformation in its AI strategy. Initially, they wanted to promote Wenxin Yiyan; last year during Baidu's 25th anniversary, Li Yanhong's speech only mentioned Baidu Wenku, but this year the core emphasis has shifted to the Baidu APP.

Baidu has also integrated DeepSeek and other models under its umbrella. This adjustment reflects Baidu's response to the market and shows the flexibility and adaptability of large tech companies in their AI strategies.

## AI and Changes in Production Relations

### Interest Distribution and Platform Conflicts

As AI agent capabilities strengthen, traditional platform ecosystems face challenges. Meituan has already blocked agent crawlers to protect its business model. This reflects the potential conflict between platforms and AI.

Our ideal AI ecosystem would have AI help us find the cheapest takeout, but platforms have their own business logic, needing to display ads and recommended content during browsing, potentially influencing user decisions. AI is not affected by these factors and will directly find the optimal solution, threatening the platform's business model.

When AI can bypass platform design logic and directly provide users with optimal choices, what will happen to platforms' advertising and recommendation mechanisms? This is essentially a change in production relations. Technology (productive forces) is developing rapidly, but the method of distributing benefits (production relations) has not kept up. In the future, new settlement mechanisms and business relationships may need to be established to balance all parties' interests.

Guests discussed that this conflict will definitely be unavoidable in the future, just like how Taobao once blocked WeChat search. It may ultimately evolve into a question of whose ecosystem is thicker.

### Google's Experience and Insights

Google faced similar issues when launching its AI Overview feature. The industry was generally concerned that this would have a huge impact on Google's advertising revenue and SEO ecosystem, with everyone asking whether the entire internet ecosystem would experience massive shocks due to this change.

However, the actual impact was not as severe as expected. According to financial report conference disclosures, it didn't significantly affect Google's advertising revenue, and Google is actively advancing the commercialization of AI Overview. High-quality content from top-ranking websites can still be included in AI Overview, so although their absolute traffic values decreased, the conversion funnel changes at the end were not significant.

This provides valuable reference for the Chinese market. China's AI application development is relatively behind the US, as "China's AI applications are still slower than those in the US because our DeepSeek only appeared during this year's Spring Festival." This means the Chinese market still has time to adjust and adapt.

## Competition Logic Between All-in-One Assistants and Vertical Applications

### The "Split Then Merge" Trend of the AI Era

The AI era shows a "split then merge" trend. In the past mobile internet era, various functions were split into independent applications; with AI's emergence, integrating multiple functions has become possible, as today with models, everyone can come together, this split then merge.

This is because AI can autonomously understand user needs and call appropriate tools, so users don't need to switch between multiple applications. The emergence of AI's subjective initiative allows products to reintegrate fragmented scenarios into a parent product, thus fulfilling the release of demand.

This integration capability is fundamentally different from the previous frame computing. In frame computing, each requirement needed developers to specifically develop programs; but now AI can organize capabilities itself, without needing to write specialized programs for every possible requirement.

### The Logic of "High Frequency Beats Low Frequency"

The logic of high frequency beats low frequency still applies in the AI era. When a platform can meet various user needs, user retention will be higher.

A guest gave an example: except for a few professionals who write presentations every day, most people might only write a presentation once a month. For these occasional users, the cost of learning professional tools is too high, so they'd rather pay for AI tools to quickly complete tasks. This is why AI is suitable for integrating multiple low-frequency capabilities to form high-frequency usage scenarios.

### Differentiation Paths for Big Companies and Startups

For large tech companies, an all-in-one assistant is a reasonable choice. If there's an opportunity today, you must choose an all-in-one, you must choose versatility. This can fully leverage their ecosystem advantages and form network effects.

For startups, the situation is different. For big companies, all-in-one is no problem, but if startups don't do all-in-one, they have no chance. Startups need to go deep in vertical domains, making their "long boards long enough" to provide professional experiences that big companies can't match.

Startups should not aim for daily active users, not try to compete with big companies' general products in terms of daily active users, but focus on Annual Revenue (AR). "In this aspect, if you can increase revenue, support your team, or eventually be acquired by a big company, that's also viable.

Someone in the podcast mentioned that this pattern hasn't it just come back around? It's becoming a few big platforms and a bunch of individuals, a bunch of creators, except those creators today have become AI creators. This reflects the cyclical nature of certain market patterns in technological revolutions.

## Browser Plugins and AI Ecosystem

### The Importance of Browser Plugins

In the specific form of AI applications, browser plugins occupy an important position, especially on the PC side. Desktop browsers remain the main entry point for users to obtain information and use services, as most people complete both information acquisition and service usage in a browser, which is actually a market formed over decades by the external industry.

Chrome's plugin ecosystem has a lot of hackable space, as it highly opens many data interfaces for user local data. This is why many AI products choose to enter through browser plugins. Manners' predecessor Monica, as well as Grammarly in its early years, both started as browser plugins and gradually developed into successful products.

### ChatGPT's Aggressive Strategy

ChatGPT took an aggressive approach when launching its Google plugin, as it can hijack Google search results. This approach was very bold, but Google couldn't directly remove it, only marking it as high risk. This demonstrates the enormous potential and possibilities of browser plugins.

### Quark's Advantages and Opportunities

As a browser-based product, Quark can directly integrate many plugin functions, such as AI summaries. This makes the user experience more smooth, without needing to install multiple plugins separately.

A guest suggested that Quark might have the opportunity to establish its own plugin ecosystem based on the "Super Frame" platform for developers. Today there are still so many wild developers, everyone can do web coding, take a code generation tool, and quickly write a plugin. If Quark could establish such an ecosystem, it would represent a very large space for imagination.

## Model Commoditization and Product Competition

### DeepSeek Effect and Model Innovation

The popularity of DeepSeek during the Spring Festival created a trend: "as long as the model capability is strong enough, with a discontinuous lead, the product doesn't matter." With powerful logical abilities, DeepSeek could quickly enter the ranks of global top-tier products.

However, this trend might have a certain subjectivity. If American media hadn't certified DeepSeek as a competitor, people might not have thought it was so excellent; it's a result of mutual pressure.

### Dialectical Relationship Between Models and Products

Microsoft's Satya Nadella once proposed the view that models have been commoditized, sparking wide discussion in the industry. Some interpreted this to mean models are no longer a core competitive advantage because everyone is the same, and thus shorted Sam Altman.

However, Sam Altman recently stated in an interview that compared to having the most advanced model (SOTA), having a product with a billion daily active users is more important. This shows that the American tech industry has reached a consensus: at the current stage of AI development, product capabilities and user scale may be more critical than model technical leadership.

This view is similar to Steve Jobs' philosophy. Engineers often build behind closed doors, thinking technical advancement is enough, but not caring whether the market will accept it. The ability to make the market accept your product is what's truly important.

### User Experience Challenges

A noteworthy user pain point is the inconsistent experience across AI products. Each of us will use Claude today, DeepSeek tomorrow, and something else the day after, requiring readjustment to different prompt styles each time. Ask the same question about Chinese PC browser market share, and every answer is different.

This problem is particularly obvious in daily use. When users need to handle different tasks, they may need to switch between multiple AI products, increasing usage costs. Products that can provide consistent, predictable experiences will be more competitive in the future.

The comparison between Claude and ChatGPT also illustrates this point. "I was amazed when I recently used Claude 3.7; its abilities are already much stronger than ordinary people, but look at its daily active users compared to ChatGPT - they differ by a factor of fifty." This reflects the importance of product positioning, brand recognition, and user experience in competition.

## Multimodal Interaction and New Ecosystem

### Rich Application Scenarios

Future AI assistants won't be limited to existing forms. Quark's head Wu Jia also mentioned that the "Super Frame isn't limited to text form; it will be able to handle multimodal tasks in the future." This opens up possibilities for various innovative applications.

A guest gave an example of a gaming scenario: when players encounter difficult levels, they typically pause the game, check guides on their phones, find relevant videos, and manually drag the progress bar to the relevant part - a cumbersome process. But future AI might be able to recognize game screens in real-time, automatically match relevant strategy videos from Bilibili, and directly push the key parts, greatly enhancing the user experience.

This multimodal capability requires AI to have "agent capabilities," not just collecting information but actively intervening and manipulating information to complete tasks assigned by users.

### The "Surround Mobile" Trend

This trend might lead to a "surround mobile" situation. "The future battlefield will be surrounding, not 1v1 duels, because mobile is too powerful; it must be a group of mid-laners surrounding, adding up to be stronger than you."

Multiple smart devices (glasses, home appliances, cars, etc.) will jointly share user time currently monopolized by mobile phones. In the future, there might emerge "a large-scale combined army method to counter mobile, possibly including glasses, images, TVs, etc."

In this multi-device environment, AI's core value will become more prominent, coordinating and connecting various devices to provide a seamless user experience. This makes Quark's proposed "Super Frame" concept more forward-looking.

### Model Context Protocol's Popularity and Prospects

The Model Context Protocol concept has become very popular recently because AI can reach agreements with external hardware and use external tools to complete complex tasks. This has become a new growth point for the industry.

Users are aesthetically fatigued with the previous question-and-answer format... I want you to do more, you can do it, so I'm willing to invest more time. This reflects the upgrade of user demands, from simple dialogue to actually solving problems.

However, a guest reminded that the popularity of Model Context Protocol is similar to the open API wave from 15 years ago. At that time, big companies were all doing open APIs, Weibo had many third-party clients, and Facebook and Twitter had similar attempts. But later most of them closed, leaving only basic functions like account login. This reminds us to pay attention to the cyclical nature and sustainability of technology trends.

## Competition for Intelligence Upper Limit

Companies will ultimately focus on improving the "intelligence upper limit" and applying intelligence to more scenarios. Whether it's super frames, deep search, or chatbots, everyone will eventually converge on one track - intelligence, how to raise the intelligence upper limit, and apply this intelligence to more scenarios, definitely not limited to mobile applications.

Quark holds a unique position in this competition. Quark is Alibaba's serious layout; this piece falling on the entire battlefield, looking at the long term, has a lot of room for imagination in terms of deduction. With both search engine genes and the resources of a large tech company, it is expected to play an important role in the competition for "intelligence upper limit."

## Conclusion

After listening to this podcast, I deeply feel that China's AI application market is undergoing unprecedented changes. Quark, Yuanbao, and Doubao represent different product lines and strategic directions, and their competition is essentially about the battle for "AI as a new generation of human-machine interface."

From search box to super frame, from content flow to intelligent dialogue, from scattered applications to unified entry, from single device to multimodal interaction, AI is reshaping the relationship between people and information, people and tasks. User expectations for AI have also transformed from simple Q&A to comprehensive intelligent assistance.

This transformation is just beginning, and we are fortunate to witness and participate in it. In the coming years, as AI capabilities continue to improve and application scenarios become increasingly rich, Taiwan and China's AI application markets will undoubtedly enter a more exciting stage of development.
