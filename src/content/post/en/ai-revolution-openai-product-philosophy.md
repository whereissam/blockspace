# The Panorama of AI Revolution: OpenAI's Product Philosophy and Technology Development Trends

In this era of rapid technological advancement, artificial intelligence undoubtedly stands as the most disruptive force. As one of the world's most influential AI companies, OpenAI is leading this transformation. Through an in-depth interview with the company's Chief Product Officer Kevin Weil, we gain insight into the internal workings of the AI industry, product development philosophy, and future directions. This article comprehensively examines this hour-and-a-half conversation, exploring the core concepts in the AI industry and their profound impact on industries and society.

## TLDR
Kevin Weil, CPO at OpenAI, shares key insights about AI development including: 
1. AI technology advances non-linearly, with significant improvements every few months; 
2. OpenAI uses "iterative deployment" and "model maximalism" philosophies; 
3. "Evals" (model evaluations) are becoming a core product development skill; 4
4. Teams use ensembles of specialized models internally; 
5. Future product teams will include researchers and ML engineers across industries; 
6. Chat remains a powerful interface for AI despite its limitations; 
7. Personalized AI tutoring has untapped potential; 
8. Vast entrepreneurial opportunities exist in industry-specific AI applications.

## I. The Fundamental Differences Between AI and Traditional Technology

### The Non-linear Nature of Technological Development

AI differs fundamentally from traditional technology sectors. In traditional companies, product teams typically innovate based on relatively stable technological foundations, with limited annual technological progress. However, the situation in the AI field is drastically different—every two months, computers can do things they've never been able to do before, requiring developers to completely rethink their approach to work.

The result of this non-linear development is staggering: the AI model you use today is the worst AI model you will ever use in your lifetime. Such rapid development creates a uniquely distinctive working environment at OpenAI.

### Balancing Ambiguity and Precision

Another key difference between AI models and traditional software lies in the nature of their inputs and outputs. Traditional software is built on the logic of precise inputs generating precise outputs, while large language models (LLMs) excel at handling the ambiguous, subtle nuances of human language but typically don't provide identical answers.

This characteristic has profound implications for product development. Unlike databases, AI models vary greatly in capability, with accuracy rates ranging from 60% to 99.5%, which directly determines the direction of product design. Therefore, deep understanding of specific use cases through "evaluations" (evals) becomes crucial for designing AI products.

## II. OpenAI's Product Philosophy and Development Methodology

### Bottom-up Decision Structure

OpenAI employs a highly decentralized decision-making model, granting teams substantial autonomy. Although the company creates quarterly and annual plans, there's no expectation that these plans will be fully realized. As Eisenhower said: "Plans are useless, but planning is indispensable."

This approach is based on recognizing the rapid pace of technological change: the value lies in the planning process rather than the final document. The planning process forces teams to pause and reflect on past achievements, lessons learned, and future dependencies, but execution often adjusts due to new discoveries.

### Iterative Deployment Strategy

OpenAI adheres to an "iterative deployment" philosophy, releasing products even before they're perfect, then continuously improving them in the public environment. This approach stems from a realization: everyone is learning about these models' capabilities together, and iterating in public accelerates the evolutionary process, allowing the company to evolve alongside the rest of society as they explore the models' characteristics.

This strategy enables the company to quickly gather user feedback, avoiding wasted time in isolation, while also involving users in the product evolution process.

### Model Maximalism

Facing model imperfections, OpenAI has adopted a stance of "model maximalism." This philosophy holds that current model deficiencies will be overcome in new models within months, so there's little need to invest heavily in building auxiliary systems to compensate for current shortcomings.

This mindset directly guides team actions: a better model will be available in two months that will overcome any current limitations. This extends as advice to developers: if your product is at the edge of current model capabilities, continue forward, as in a few months, improved models will transform your product.

## III. Evaluations (Evals): The Core Skill in AI Product Development

### The Nature and Importance of Evaluations

"Evaluations" (evals) are becoming a core skill for product managers. These evaluations are essentially tests for models, similar to student exams, measuring model performance on specific tasks. Creating effective evaluations is crucial for developing successful AI products for three reasons:

1. **Quantification of performance differences**: Accuracy rates vary significantly across different tasks, directly affecting product design approaches.
2. **Foundation for continuous improvement**: Evaluations provide objective standards for measuring progress.
3. **Domain-specific adaptability**: Most enterprise data and processes aren't public, requiring customized evaluations.

### The Deep Research Example

OpenAI's "Deep Research" feature provides an excellent case study of evaluations in product development. This feature allows users to submit complex queries, with ChatGPT spending 25-30 minutes conducting in-depth research, completing work that might take a user a week.

During development, the team designed evaluations targeting key use cases, then continuously optimized the model through these evaluations. Improvements in model performance on these evaluations directly validated the product's viability.

## IV. Internal Applications of AI and Product Team Transformation

### The Power of Model Ensembles

OpenAI extensively employs "model ensembles" to solve problems internally. For 10 different problems, the team might use 20 different model calls, some using specialized fine-tuned models. They use different-sized models based on varying latency or cost requirements for different problems.

This approach can be likened to human team collaboration: a company is essentially a collection of fine-tuned models, each member possessing different skills based on educational background and professional experience. These models combine in various configurations, with the overall output far superior to that of any individual.

### The Rise of Vibe Coding

In terms of working methods, "vibe coding" is gradually becoming a new trend—a method that lets AI lead the coding process. Traditionally, developers would give a model a prompt, let it generate code, then edit manually. But as model capabilities improve, developers can "let go," allowing the model to continuously generate code, providing guidance only when necessary.

An impressive example is how OpenAI's Chief People Officer rebuilt an internal tool she had used at her previous company through vibe coding. This demonstrates that AI can now assist non-technical professionals in meaningful development work.

### Future Composition of Product Teams

The structure of future product teams will change significantly, with researchers likely integrated into every product team. This trend extends beyond foundation model companies to all industries, as fine-tuned models become ubiquitous.

AI will be omnipresent like transistors, becoming part of everything we do. Numerous fine-tuned models will emerge, as customizing models for specific use cases offers clear advantages. Consequently, almost every team will need quasi-researcher/machine learning engineer types, as fine-tuning models becomes a core workflow in building most products.

## V. The Future and Advantages of Chat Interfaces

### Chat as a Universal Interface

Regarding the future of AI interfaces, a perspective contradicting mainstream views deserves attention. While many believe chat isn't the ultimate form of AI interaction, chat may actually be an exceptionally effective interface due to its universal nature.

Chat essentially reflects humans' most natural form of communication—unstructured exchange. If restricted to more rigid interfaces, communication bandwidth would be drastically reduced. The uniqueness of large language models lies in their ability to understand the complexity and nuances of human language, making chat the perfect interface to match these models' capabilities.

This doesn't mean chat is the only interface. For high-frequency, specific use cases, more structured interfaces might be more suitable. However, chat remains the baseline for handling any issues beyond specific domain boundaries.

## VI. OpenAI's Internal Operations and Talent Strategy

### Synergy Between Research and Product

OpenAI has transformed from a pure research company to one balancing product and research. ChatGPT initially was just a low-key research preview, not expected to become a massive product. As it grew, the company gradually strengthened its product capabilities while maintaining a firm stance: OpenAI should never become a purely product company but needs to simultaneously be a world-class research company and world-class product company.

The challenge in this transformation lies in effective collaboration between research and product teams. If completely separated, the product team essentially becomes just an API consumer of its own models. The best products come from close collaboration: understanding problems to be solved, establishing evaluations for these problems, and using these evaluations to collect data and fine-tune models.

### Product Manager Roles and Recruitment Standards

OpenAI's product manager team is relatively lean, with about 25 members. This setup is based on a philosophy: organizations should minimize the number of PMs. Too many PMs create problems, generating ideas rather than execution.

In recruitment standards, three key qualities are particularly important:
1. **High autonomy**: Seeing and solving problems without waiting for instructions.
2. **Embracing ambiguity**: Adapting to highly uncertain environments.
3. **Leading through influence**: Especially when working with highly autonomous research teams.

Decision-making ability is equally crucial: when ambiguity exists and no one is making decisions, product managers need to ensure the team makes decisions and moves forward.

## VII. The Combination of Education and Artificial Intelligence

### The Potential of Personalized Tutoring

Personalized tutoring could be one of AI's most important applications. Research data shows that when classrooms combine with personalized tutoring, learning speed increases by multiple standard deviations. This is an evidence-based educational improvement, and tools like ChatGPT are freely available.

Notably, widespread AI tutoring systems have yet to emerge. People worldwide have smart devices, and AI tutoring has the potential to transform the educational landscape, but this potential remains largely untapped.

## VIII. Lessons from the Libra Project

### The Greatest Disappointment of a Career

The Libra (cryptocurrency) project at Facebook is described as "the biggest disappointment of my career." The product's failure to exist in the world today is deeply regrettable, as it could have made the world better.

The Libra project aimed to solve pain points in the global remittance market. Its vision was: users could send small amounts of money instantly and free of charge in WhatsApp, with everyone having an account balance. However, the project faced multiple challenges, including:

1. **Excessive ambition**: Attempting to accomplish everything at once, including launching a new blockchain, a basket of currencies, and integration with messaging apps.
2. **Company reputation issues**: The project coincided with Facebook's reputation being at its lowest point.

Although the technological foundation still exists in two companies, Aptos and Miston, the vision of making remittances as simple and inexpensive as sending text messages remains unfulfilled. Given the current administration's friendliness toward cryptocurrency and Meta's improved reputation, perhaps now would be a good time to reconsider the project.

## IX. Industry Outlook and Entrepreneurial Opportunities

### Areas Large Model Companies Won't Enter

Addressing entrepreneurs' concerns about "which areas OpenAI won't enter," an important insight comes from former Twitter CEO Ev Williams: no matter how large a company is, there are always more smart people outside its walls than inside.

For this reason, OpenAI focuses on building a quality API, currently used by 3 million developers. No matter how ambitious they are, large AI companies cannot possibly have sufficient manpower and expertise to build all possible AI applications. Moreover, these companies typically don't pursue unlimited expansion.

This creates vast opportunity spaces for entrepreneurs, especially behind industry-specific and use-case-specific data typically hidden behind company firewalls.

### AI's Impact on Economy and Employment

Regarding concerns about job displacement by AI, technology optimists offer an important perspective: looking back over centuries, technology has driven economic progress, geopolitical advancement, quality of life improvements, and longevity. Technology forms the foundation of almost all progress.

This doesn't mean there won't be temporary dislocations or individual impacts. However, it's worth noting that ChatGPT is also one of the best retraining tools, capable of teaching people new skills to help them adapt to change.

## X. Prompt Engineering and AI Usage Techniques

### Learning Through Examples

Regarding prompt engineering, an important perspective exists: ideally, users shouldn't need to become prompt engineers, just as most people don't need to deeply understand database storage engine details. If AI companies do their job well, this goal should be achievable.

However, at the current stage, a particularly effective prompting strategy is including examples. This resembles "poor man's fine-tuning": including examples and corresponding good answers in your prompt, then asking the model to solve similar problems. The model learns from these examples and applies this learning to new problems.

Another effective technique is assigning the model a specific identity, such as asking it to answer physics questions as Einstein or solve branding problems as a top marketing expert. This approach guides the model toward a specific mindset, producing better results.

## Conclusion: The Future Landscape of Artificial Intelligence

The insights from OpenAI's Chief Product Officer Kevin Weil reveal multiple key dimensions of artificial intelligence development. From product philosophy to the importance of evaluations, from the power of model ensembles to future changes in product team structure, these observations not only reflect the cutting-edge thinking in the current AI field but also outline a picture of future work and life.

A motto worth remembering is: sometimes it's not any one thing, just good work consistently over a long period of time. Success often comes not from a single breakthrough but from continuous accumulation. In this rapidly evolving AI era, this wisdom is particularly valuable. We stand at the beginning of a new era of human-machine collaboration, where individuals and organizations that can effectively use AI to enhance rather than replace human capabilities will reap the greatest benefits from this technological revolution.
