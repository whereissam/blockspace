## w3.group: Ecosystem Builder for Web3

Founded in 2022 with headquarters in Berlin, w3.group is dedicated to accelerating Web3 development, a new phase of the internet emphasizing decentralization, blockchain technology, and user sovereignty. They integrate funding, space, and educational resources to create an open collaborative platform connecting global entrepreneurs and technology enthusiasts. Unlike traditional companies, w3.group operates on four pillars:

- **w3.fund**: Provides funding for Web3 startups, focusing on DeFi (Decentralized Finance), NFTs (Non-Fungible Tokens), and DAOs (Decentralized Autonomous Organizations).
- **w3.hub**: A shared space in central Berlin that combines office space and events, forming the core of community exchange.
- **w3.vision**: Bridges Web3 and the public through media and education.
- **w3.labs**: Offers staking and compliance tools, bridging traditional finance and the decentralized world.

These pillars collaborate with each other: projects invested in by w3.fund may be implemented at w3.hub and then marketed through w3.vision. This model has attracted participants from Europe, North America, and Asia, demonstrating global influence.

## w3.fund: The Funding Engine for Innovative Projects

w3.fund is the investment pillar of the w3.group ecosystem, established in 2022 in Berlin, Germany. As a venture capital fund focused on the Web3 domain, w3.fund positions itself as a "Web3 native investment company," adopting a specialized investment model for blockchain startups.

w3.fund's investment strategy has the following distinct characteristics:

- **Hybrid Investment Approach**: Combines equity and token investments, focusing on pre-seed and seed rounds to meet the unique financing needs of Web3 projects.
- **Web3 Application Layer Focus**: Prioritizes support for tokenization, Web3 x AI, gaming, DeFi, financial services, and B2B use cases.
- **Investment Scope Planning**: Aims to invest in dozens of startups, primarily in Europe while also covering other global regions.
- **Portfolio**: Has invested in 19 Web3 startups

### w3.fund's Ecosystem Approach

w3.fund is not just an independent investment entity but also an important component of w3.group's integrated ecosystem. Its collaborative methods include:

- **Ecosystem Synergy**: Invested projects can utilize w3.hub's physical space for collaboration and promote results through w3.vision.
- **Community-Driven**: Connects portfolio companies with the broader Web3 community through w3.hub events.
- **Technical Support**: Provides technical resources to help portfolio companies develop blockchain applications.

This model creates a comprehensive support system for startups, from funding to community.

### Expansion and Strategic Partnerships: Building Web3 Bridges

w3.fund actively expands its influence, for example, collaborating with Celestia to host modular blockchain workshops during Berlin Blockchain Week 2024, promoting the adoption of its data availability layer by German projects.

Additionally, in November 2024, w3.fund launched w3.wave, a crypto fund focused on liquid token investments, managed by Julius Nagel and Philipp Beer, with a minimum investment of 100,000 euros, targeting semi-professional and professional investors.

These initiatives show that w3.fund not only provides funding but also promotes Web3 development in Germany and Europe through partnerships and innovation.

## w3.vision: Bringing Web3 into Our Daily Lives

w3.vision is part of the w3.group ecosystem, a community-owned media network focused on Web3, headquartered in Berlin, Germany. Its core mission is to create an innovative business media platform through decentralized and transparent processes, placing participants at the core of interests, and promoting the adoption and understanding of Web3 technology.

They regularly host large events and conferences. In 2023, they organized a Web3 conference in Cologne, Germany, attracting both newcomers and experts to discuss future possibilities. In early 2025, they launched an 8-week "Digital Assets Season" in Zurich, London, and other cities, allowing businesses and the public to experience Web3 applications up close.

They've also created Onchain Culture, a platform focused on showcasing how Web3 is changing various industries. For instance, it might introduce how blockchain helps farmers track crop sales or enables fashion brands to launch digital clothing, bringing technology closer to daily life. Their goal is to make Web3 not just a topic for experts but a future everyone can participate in.

## w3.labs: The Technical Partner Making Blockchain More Accessible

w3.labs is the technical department of w3.group, focused on developing tools that make blockchain technology simpler and more reliable for ordinary people and businesses. One of their main services is "staking," which helps you earn returns by placing digital assets into blockchain networks, similar to earning interest from bank deposits but more secure and transparent.

w3.labs offers several core services:

- **Staking Services**: Helps you earn returns by placing digital currencies into blockchain networks, similar to earning interest from bank deposits but with more transparency and automatic generation of tax documents, saving complicated procedures.
- **Digital Wallets**: Designs secure storage tools allowing businesses and individuals to easily manage their digital assets, such as stablecoins or Bitcoin, without worrying about theft or loss.
- **Payment Solutions**: Develops fast cross-border payment systems, such as enabling companies to pay international bills using blockchain technology, faster and cheaper than traditional bank transfers.
- **Compliance Tools**: Provides technical support compliant with German law, such as automatic transaction reporting, helping businesses use blockchain confidently under strict regulations.

Their target clients are mainly traditional financial institutions, like banks, aiming to help these major players smoothly enter the Web3 world. For example, a German bank might use w3.labs' services to allow customers to pay directly with stablecoins on their phones, securely and time-efficiently. In the future, w3.labs hopes to create a standardized technology that makes blockchain as simple and widespread as email.

## w3.wave: The Investment Window for Digital Currencies

### Market Positioning and Basic Features

The w3.group ecosystem welcomed its newest member in November 2024, w3.wave, a cryptocurrency fund focused on liquid token investments. Unlike w3.fund's approach of focusing on early-stage startup equity investments, w3.wave targets already listed crypto tokens with liquidity, aiming to achieve investment performance superior to Bitcoin through active management strategies.

w3.wave is managed by Julius Nagel and Philipp Beer, with the fund clearly positioned to serve semi-professional and professional investor groups, setting a minimum investment threshold of 100,000 euros. This positioning reflects w3.wave's professional orientation in the crypto investment field, intended to attract participants with certain investment experience and capital strength.

### Investment Philosophy and Operational Model

w3.wave's core investment philosophy is built on the unique "early liquidity events" characteristic of the cryptocurrency market. This feature allows investors to obtain returns similar to venture capital while not having to sacrifice liquidity like traditional VC. This model of high return potential combined with liquidity is the core of w3.wave's value proposition.

w3.wave adopts an actively managed investment approach, meaning the team conducts market research and asset screening rather than passively tracking indices. This active management strategy aims to help investors achieve excess returns in the complex and volatile crypto market, especially compared to simply holding benchmark assets like Bitcoin.

### Regulatory Compliance and Structural Design

As a fund regulated in Germany, w3.wave particularly emphasizes its compliance advantages. The team spent years designing a "tax-efficient" fund structure in Germany. This feature is especially attractive to German and European investors planning to enter the crypto market, as it addresses the common tax complexities in crypto investments.

w3.wave's regulated status provides additional peace of mind for investors, especially in a crypto currency market often viewed as having unclear regulations. This formalized investment channel allows traditional investors to participate in crypto asset investments in a familiar and compliant manner.

### Synergies with the w3.group Ecosystem

w3.wave does not operate in isolation but works closely with the entire w3.group ecosystem. w3.wave's collaborative relationship with the w3.fund ecosystem leverages various synergies to enhance its investment capabilities.

This integration provides w3.wave with unique advantages, allowing it to leverage w3.group's extensive network and resources to gain market insights, technical trends, and investment opportunities. In a fast-developing and information-intensive market like cryptocurrency, this ecosystem synergy could become an important competitive advantage.

w3.wave represents an important strategic expansion of w3.group into the crypto secondary market, adding a new dimension to its ecosystem. By providing regulated liquid crypto asset investment channels, w3.wave complements w3.fund's early investment focus, allowing w3.group to participate more comprehensively in the crypto asset value chain, from early project incubation to mature token trading, achieving full market coverage.

## w3.hub: The Convergence of Physical and Digital Innovation

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fb9abaa67-a210-4260-aa77-8f03c4553442_1280x960.jpeg)

w3.hub is a physical base created by w3.group in central Berlin, with a total area of 1,500 square meters, including shared workspaces, meeting rooms, and relaxation areas. This is not just an office location but also an activity center for the Web3 community. Visitors can book entry by paying a daily ticket of 19 euros (tax not included) and experience the fusion of traditional architecture with new technology, such as using a mobile phone to scan codes to receive digital souvenirs (like NFTs).

w3.hub's space design blends historical and modern essences. The original high ceilings, large windows, and exposed brick walls preserve the building's vintage charm, while high-speed fiber optics, advanced projection systems, and smart office facilities inject a technological atmosphere. From strategically placed public lounges to modularly configured meeting rooms, every detail is designed to inspire chance encounters and creative collisions, nurturing community awareness and a spirit of collective innovation.

In 2024, w3.hub hosted multiple events, including technical seminars, corporate training, and community gatherings. For example, during Berlin Blockchain Week 2024, they collaborated with Solana to host a 24-hour hackathon attracting 65 developers, with results displayed on large screens, highlighting the potential of Web3 technology.

w3.hub's operations use smart contracts to manage the booking process and had previously launched an "Innovator Sales" strategy offering early bird discounted tickets. In 2023, they upgraded their technical systems, adding NFT airdrop claim functionality and optimizing meeting room configurations to accommodate different event needs. The design here aims to promote creative exchange, transforming Web3 concepts into practical applications.
