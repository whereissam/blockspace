# "Indie Hacker" Conversation: Website Management and SEO Strategies

## Introduction

The [Indie Hacker podcast](https://open.spotify.com/episode/1qui6PAOfZz9Mk5qvCOfjM?si=nwPT8ZMuQsm8INkPLYNuSw) recently invited renowned SEO expert Brother Fei to share insights on using websites as a lifelong career. Brother Fei has over ten years of SEO experience, having started learning and practicing SEO techniques since 2007, initially gaining traffic from Baidu search engine, and now focusing on Google traffic. Below is a summary of their conversation.

## Brother Fei's Entrepreneurial Journey

Brother Fei's website journey began during his university years when, to earn living expenses, he learned about website construction through campus distribution of "Speech and Eloquence" magazine. To improve sales efficiency, he taught himself website creation and learned SEO and traffic acquisition strategies from platforms like China Webmaster Site and A5 China Webmaster Network.

Initially, he promoted through campus forums and bulletin boards, but such advertising content was easily deleted. Therefore, he turned to SEO strategies to obtain free and stable traffic through search engines. By 2008, he was already able to earn one to two hundred dollars monthly from his website, which was considerable income for a student.

After graduation, Brother Fei started a business developing apps with friends, but the apps never became profitable. To improve the team's living conditions, he managed app backend development while creating websites to earn advertising fees to support the team. Additionally, they took on H5 outsourcing projects, receiving orders worth more than 2 million yuan in over a year.

Later, the team disbanded, with members going in different directions—some went into mobile gaming, others into quantitative trading, while Brother Fei continued to focus on website-related business, completely abandoning app development.

## Brother Fei's Core Competitiveness

Brother Fei believes his two major core advantages are:

1. **Traffic Generation Ability**: Particularly skilled at obtaining free traffic
2. **Fast Development Speed**: Can launch a website in as little as two hours

He emphasizes that many products don't need months of fine-tuning; as long as they meet users' core needs, have a reasonably attractive interface, and smooth basic interaction, users will accept them. Prioritizing quick product launch, acquiring users, and then continuously improving based on feedback is a more feasible strategy.

## Opportunity Discovery Methodology

### Deep Understanding of Google's Relationship with Webmasters

Brother Fei emphasizes that there exists a symbiotic ecological relationship between Google and webmasters. As the world's largest search engine, Google relies on the rich web content, information services, functional applications, and e-commerce services provided by webmasters worldwide to meet various search needs of users globally. The vast majority of search results come from third-party websites, not Google's own pages.

Meanwhile, webmasters need Google to provide continuous, stable free traffic to support website operations. This mutually beneficial, complementary relationship is the foundation for understanding the essence of SEO. Brother Fei believes that the core value of webmasters lies in helping Google better meet user needs, which is also the key to SEO success.

### Finding Low-Competition, High-Demand Search Terms in Multiple Dimensions

Brother Fei shared a systematic approach to opportunity discovery, with the following detailed strategy steps:

#### 1. In-depth Analysis of Web Page Supply

When searching for specific keywords on Google, the search results page displays an estimated number of web pages, representing the total number of pages related to that keyword. Brother Fei suggests paying special attention to keywords with relatively fewer web pages, as this indicates smaller market competition, making it easier for newcomers to gain rankings.

However, judging a keyword's value solely by low web page supply is insufficient. Few web pages might also mean low demand, so analysis should combine other dimensions.

#### 2. Comprehensive Google Trends Analysis

Google Trends is a free and powerful analytical tool that integrates user search behavior, media coverage volume, and other dimensions to provide normalized data for keyword popularity. Brother Fei recommends the following trend analysis for keywords:

- **Long-term Trend Observation**: Review keyword popularity charts over the past 12 months or longer
- **Trend Judgment**: Analyze whether the term is in a stable state, steadily rising, or declining
- **Trend Nature Assessment**: Generally avoid declining trend terms; stable trend terms indicate continuing demand; rising trends indicate growth potential

Brother Fei particularly emphasizes that he uses known website traffic data as a baseline and compares it with newly discovered terms in Google Trends to estimate the true search volume of new terms. For example, if a newly discovered term has twice the value of a known term in Google Trends, its actual search volume is likely in the same magnitude.
