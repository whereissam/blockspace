## How Blockchain Breaks Traditional Financing Rules

### Key Terminology

- **Capital Increase**: The act of a company increasing its capital to expand operations, repay debt, or increase funds
- **Convertible Loan**: A type of loan that can be converted into company equity under specific conditions, typically provided by investors to startups
- **ESOP & VSOP**: Both are employee incentive programs designed to allow employees to share in the company's growth, increasing loyalty and performance
- **ESOP**: Employee Stock Ownership Plan, employees have the right to purchase company stock, own company shares, and enjoy shareholder rights
- **VSOP**: Virtual Stock Option Plan, employees have no actual equity but can enjoy benefits from stock price appreciation. Employees have no substantial equity, only receiving benefits when the company is sold or goes public
- **Dry Income**: Unrealized income generated from stock appreciation or equity plans, which requires tax payments despite not receiving cash

## Disrupting Tradition: The Innovative Model of Tokenized Company Equity

The core concept of Tokenize.it is to "tokenize" traditional company equity. Using Ethereum blockchain technology, they developed GmbH tokens (Gesellschaft mit beschränkter Haftung, abbreviated as GmbH, meaning German limited liability company), which represent profit participation rights in a company.

### How GmbH Tokens Work: Cracking the German Legal Challenge

#### Legal Barriers and Structural Problems in Traditional German Company Financing

To understand Tokenize.it's innovation, one must first deeply understand the strict legal limitations and structural problems facing GmbH equity financing in Germany. These challenges create enormous barriers for startup companies in practice:

**High Transaction Costs and Complicated Processes**: According to German law, any GmbH equity transfer must go through a notarization process, with notary fees typically 1-1.5% of the transaction amount. Adding lawyer fees and administrative costs makes small investments economically unfeasible. An investment of just €10,000 might require hundreds of euros in notary and legal fees. Additionally, the notarization process requires investors and founders to be physically present, which is particularly inconvenient for international investors.

**Rigid Financing Structure**: Traditional financing models typically center around "funding rounds," where founders need to gather all investors, determine valuation, and complete legal processes within a specific timeframe. This model not only consumes a lot of time (typically 3-6 months to complete a financing round) but also forces founders to shift their focus from business to fundraising, creating enormous pressure before cash runs out.

**Diversity and Complexity of Asset Types**: In traditional company structures, different stakeholders hold different types of financial instruments: investors own shares, early supporters might hold convertible notes, while employees participate through ESOP or VSOP programs. These heterogeneous instruments have varying terms, creating management complexity and lack of transparency, increasing the difficulty of corporate governance.

**Barriers to Employee Participation**: Traditional ESOPs require notarization and registration with the commercial register, resulting in high administrative costs. Employees receiving shares may face "dry income" tax issues, requiring taxes on unrealized gains. While VSOPs avoid these problems, they typically lack liquidity, only realizable when the company exits, reducing their incentive effect.

**Severe Lack of Liquidity**: GmbH shares have extremely low liquidity under German legal frameworks, with investors typically only able to exit when the company is acquired or goes public. This long-term lack of liquidity increases investment risk and limits the range of potential investors.

**Limitations on Community Participation**: Due to high legal and administrative costs, traditional GmbHs find it difficult to accept small investments from customers, users, or supporters. This limits a company's ability to build an owner community, losing important marketing and user loyalty advantages.

Additionally, German law imposes strict restrictions on public fundraising, with public offerings typically requiring prospectus submission, which is costly and complicated. Equity changes must also be updated in the German Commercial Register (Handelsregister), a time-consuming bureaucratic procedure. These compound factors make the traditional GmbH financing process slow, expensive, and inflexible.

#### Tokenize.it's Innovative Legal Solution

Facing these multi-level challenges, Tokenize.it developed an innovative legal and technical framework that fundamentally reshapes the company financing model:

On the technical level, GmbH tokens are based on the Ethereum blockchain, following the ERC-20 token standard, ensuring the security, uniqueness, and transferability of tokens. All smart contracts used to create tokens are open-source and have undergone rigorous review by professional audit institutions.

On the legal level, Tokenize.it cleverly utilizes two key mechanisms in German law to circumvent traditional barriers:

**Profit Participation Rights Framework**: Defining tokens as "profit participation rights" (Genussrechte) rather than actual equity. This is a special financial instrument in German law that is not subject to the notarization requirements of the Limited Liability Company Act, allowing the transfer of economic interests without notarization.

**Public Reward Mechanism**: Adopting the concept of "public reward" (Auslobung) from the German Civil Code, which is a unilateral legal promise. The company publicly announces that those who hold specific tokens and sign specific transactions will receive economic benefits (such as dividends, exit proceeds, etc.), creating a legally enforceable promise without requiring both parties to sign complex contracts.

This dual legal framework, combined with blockchain technology, allows Tokenize.it to provide a system that is both regulatory compliant and highly efficient. Investors can complete a fully digital investment process in minutes, without needing to attend any meetings in person or pay notary fees. This makes small investments (as low as €200) possible, greatly expanding the potential investor base.

The platform also provides standardized contracts reviewed by legal experts, greatly reducing legal fees and negotiation time, simplifying the entire investment process while ensuring legal compliance and protection of all parties' rights.

#### Specific Rights and Limitations of Token Holders

GmbH token holders enjoy a series of economic rights that put them on equal footing with actual shareholders in terms of economic benefits:

**Economic Rights**: Holders can participate in the company's economic success, including dividends, exit proceeds, and liquidation proceeds, enjoying the same financial returns as actual shareholders.

**Information Rights**: Holders have the right to access limited information such as the company's annual financial statements, maintaining basic understanding and oversight of their investment.

**Transferability**: Tokens can be freely transferred within the Ethereum ecosystem (companies can choose to enable an allowlist to restrict specific transfers), providing potential liquidity channels.

**"Sell Option" Mechanism**: Holders can return tokens to the company at specific times (typically once per quarter), with the company deciding how to compensate:
- Pay euros at current market value
- Provide real company shares (requiring notarization)
- Provide shares if the company converts to an AG (public company)

However, this model also has some key limitations that need to be clarified:

**No Voting Rights**: Token holders have no company governance voting rights (unless converted to actual shares)

**Conversion Restrictions**: Converting tokens to actual shares still requires notarization

**Regulatory Considerations**: Tokens are viewed as securities, requiring compliance with financial regulatory requirements (such as PRIIPs Basic Information Document) when issued to the public

Through this innovative legal and technical framework, Tokenize.it has achieved a fundamental transformation of the traditional GmbH financing model, solving the three core challenges of efficiency, cost, and liquidity, opening up a new financing path for startup companies in Germany and even Europe.

## Three Core Products: Reshaping Investment and Participation Methods

Based on the innovative legal framework of GmbH tokens, Tokenize.it has developed three main services, each targeting a specific market need:

### 1. Private Fundraise

Targeted at startups looking to raise funds from investors (such as angel investors, friends, and family). Founders can generate investment links through the platform and send them to potential investors. Investors only need to click the link, accept the terms, and transfer funds to complete the investment. The entire process does not require notary involvement, greatly saving time and costs.

### 2. Employee Participation

Allows companies to provide company equity to employees as a way to incentivize and retain talent. Compared to traditional employee stock option plans (ESOP) or virtual stock option plans (VSOP), Tokenize.it's solution is more flexible and convenient. Companies can set vesting plans for employees and grant profit participation rights to employees in the form of tokens.

### 3. Crowdinvesting

Allows companies to raise funds from public investors, with amounts up to €8 million. Companies can embed an "Invest Now" button on their websites, allowing a broader group of investors to participate in investment. The platform provides necessary legal documents, such as the PRIIPs Basic Information Document (BIB) required by the German financial regulatory authority BaFin.
